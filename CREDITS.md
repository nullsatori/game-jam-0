# Third-party sources

Everything in `Content/MindKore` is MindKore's own content (meshes, materials,
textures, data assets, Niagara, synthesised sounds), except the USP pistol
(a CC BY model reworked in MindKore, see Models). The sounds below are cut
from third-party recordings; the entries are copied from MindKore's
`Docs/CREDITS.md`. No content pack (Fab, Marketplace) is included.

## Textures

`Content/JamArt/Textures/Flesh/T_Jam_Gritty_*` (muscle, tendon, vessels, wetness,
bundle end, membrane, planet skin, planet eye, suckers) are the owner's own
AI generations (17.10.2026, a gritty retro palette after Half-Life 1, Postal 2
and Buckshot Roulette; no texture from those games was used). Imported at
256 px. The normal maps `T_Jam_Gritty_*_N` (1024 px) are derived from them by
`Scripts/make_flesh_normals.py`. The frame of the mesh living wall
uses MindKore's own `T_MK_Yard_Rust_BC/N`.

The meshes in `Content/JamArt/Meshes` (the planetoid, its eye and tentacle, the living wall, the flyer) are generated by the project's own Blender scripts (`Scripts/blender/`); no third-party model is used. The flyer's colour atlas `Content/JamArt/Textures/Flyer/T_Jam_Flyer_BC` is painted procedurally by the same script (`Scripts/blender/jam_flyer.py`).

## Models

**Low poly USP-S** — by **Victorcstr**, via Sketchfab.
Licence: [CC Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Commercial use permitted; **attribution required**.
In the jam: `/Game/MindKore/Weapons/USP/SM_MK_USP_Soviet` with
`M_MK_USP_Soviet` and `T_MK_USP_BaseColor/ORM/NormalDX` (2048 px), copied byte
for byte from MindKore, where its enemies carry it. Modified from the original
in MindKore (24.09.2026, `Docs/Art/USP_20260924/MODEL.md` there): scaled to
real-world size, origin moved to the grip, a `Muzzle` socket added, the frame
and slide reworked, Bakelite grips with a stamped «17», MindKore's own baked
maps. Entry copied from MindKore's `Docs/CREDITS.md`. The jam's pistol in first
person.

**The pump shotgun** (`/Game/JamArt/Meshes/Weapons/SM_Jam_Shotgun`) is
MindKore's own: the 92 cm Soviet pump gun that MindKore's hero carries on his
back (concept 01, built from primitives by MindKore's `gun_r17.py`), rebuilt
in gun space by `Scripts/blender/jam_guns.py` and given plain colour
materials (`/Game/JamArt/Materials/Weapons`). No third-party model.

**The SV-98** (`/Game/JamArt/Meshes/Weapons/SM_Jam_SV98`) is built by the same
script from the same primitives after the real rifle's silhouette (public
descriptions of its proportions only); no third-party model.

The tracer material `/Game/MindKore/Weapons/M_MK_Tracer` is MindKore's own.

The muzzle flash and powder smoke (`/Game/JamArt/FX/M_Jam_MuzzleFlash`,
`M_Jam_MuzzleSmoke`) are the jam's own: procedural materials built by
`Scripts/jam_muzzle_fx.py`, no texture, on the engine plane. MindKore's
`NS_MK_MuzzleFlash` was not taken: it uses the Vefects Free_Fire pack (Fab)
and a FootageCrate frame.
