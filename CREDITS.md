# Third-party sources

Everything in `Content/MindKore` is MindKore's own content (meshes, materials,
textures, data assets, Niagara, synthesised sounds). The sounds below are cut
from third-party recordings; the entries are copied from MindKore's
`Docs/CREDITS.md`. No content pack (Fab, Marketplace) is included.

## Textures

`Content/JamArt/Textures/Flesh/T_Jam_Gritty_*` (muscle, tendon, vessels, wetness,
bundle end, membrane, planet skin, planet eye, suckers) are the owner's own
AI generations (17.10.2026, a gritty retro palette after Half-Life 1, Postal 2
and Buckshot Roulette; no texture from those games was used). Imported at
256 px. The normal maps `T_Jam_Gritty_*_N` (1024 px) are derived from them by
`Scripts/make_flesh_normals.py`. The frame of the mesh living wall
uses MindKore's own `T_MK_Yard_Rust_BC/N`.

The meshes in `Content/JamArt/Meshes` (the planetoid, its eye and tentacle, the living wall) are generated by the project's own Blender scripts (`Scripts/blender/`); no third-party model is used.

## Sky

Star map: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020 (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851). Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC; Hipparcos, Tycho-2: ESA. Via Wikimedia Commons, attribution required.

Used as `Content/JamArt/Textures/Sky/T_Jam_Starmap_Gal` (galactic projection, equirectangular; downscaled from 3840 x 1920 to 1024 x 512 keeping each block's brightest star, lightly graded and posterized by `Scripts/make_lowres_starmap.py`) on the sky dome of `Lvl_Jam_Ship`.
